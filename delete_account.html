Account Deletion Request

If you would like to delete your Shop Yanga account and all associated data, please send an email to:

info@codemala.com

Include the email address used to create your account.

Data that will be deleted

  • User account information
  • Inventory records
  • Sales transactions
  • Business profile information

Data retention

Some financial transaction records may be retained for legal or accounting purposes for up to 30 days before permanent deletion.
